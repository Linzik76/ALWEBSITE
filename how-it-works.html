Smart matching engine

Real-time matching

From vacancy to interview

  1. 1

    Employer posts a vacancy

    The role, team and company culture are captured.

  2. 2

    We analyse the role

    Our system works out what success looks like in this position.

  3. 3

    Every candidate is scored

    A success probability is calculated for each candidate in under 50ms.

  4. 4

    Top matches are shown

    The employer sees the best matches, with the reasons explained.

  5. 5

    Interviews go ahead

    The employer contacts candidates and interviews proceed as normal.

  6. 6

    Outcomes make it smarter

    Placement results feed back to improve future matches.

Our roadmap

Where we are headed

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4

Manual pilot

Validate demand and build initial cohorts of 50–100 candidates with one ETB or large employer.

Phase 2 · Weeks 5–16

MVP

Rules-based matching, readiness modules, employer vacancies and launch of apprenticelinks.ie.

Phase 3 · Weeks 17–32

Machine learning

Full matching engine, personality and learning style assessments, and outcome dashboards.

Phase 4 · Weeks 33–48

Scale

SOLAS and ETB integration, mentor matching, UK expansion and an alumni network.
