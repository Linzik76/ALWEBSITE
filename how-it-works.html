How It Works

Smart matching, a readiness programme and employer partnerships, working together for placements that succeed.

The Three Pillars

ApprenticeLinks is built on three interconnected pillars that work together to create successful apprenticeship placements:

1. Smart Matching Engine

Our AI matching algorithm analyzes 42 data points about each candidate and vacancy to predict placement success with over 75% accuracy.

Candidate Features (30): Technical skills, soft skills, personality (Big Five), learning style, location preferences, readiness score, engagement level, career goals, and more.

Vacancy Features (12): Role type, sector, experience level, salary range, employer success rate, team size, company culture, required certifications, and more.

Output: A success probability (0-100) for each candidate-vacancy pairing, plus SHAP-powered explanations in plain English.

Model: XGBoost with fairness monitoring to ensure demographic parity and no algorithmic bias.

2. Readiness Programme

Before matching, candidates complete our 9-module readiness programme:

  • Apprenticeship 101
  • CV Development
  • Interview Prep
  • Communication Skills
  • Workplace Readiness
  • Digital Skills
  • Employer Research
  • Personal Branding
  • Soft Skills Assessment

Each module includes video content, exercises, quizzes, and self-reflection. Progress is tracked, and candidates earn readiness scores that feed into matching.

3. Employer Partnership & Outcomes

After placement, we track outcomes to continuously improve matches:

  • Placement Monitoring: Track apprentice progress, engagement, and learning outcomes
  • Completion Prediction: Identify candidates at risk of early termination and intervene early
  • Success Metrics: Measure long-term retention, progression to higher qualifications, and employer satisfaction
  • Model Improvement: Use outcomes data to refine our matching algorithm monthly

The Matching Process

Phase 1: Manual Pilot (Weeks 1-4) - We validate demand and build initial candidate cohorts manually with one ETB or large employer (50-100 candidates).

Phase 2: MVP (Weeks 5-16) - Launch rules-based matching. Candidates complete readiness; employers post vacancies; we manually match via dashboard. Deploy apprenticelinks.ie.

Phase 3: ML (Weeks 17-32) - Deploy XGBoost matching engine. Candidates take personality and learning style assessments. Expand employer dashboard with outcome tracking.

Phase 4: Scale (Weeks 33-48) - Integrate SOLAS and ETB systems. Add mentor matching. Launch UK expansion. Build alumni network.

Real-Time Matching

Once deployed, the matching engine works in real time:

  1. Employer posts a vacancy
  2. Our system analyzes the role and company
  3. For each candidate, we compute a success probability (<50ms)
  4. Top matches are shown to employer; candidate reasons are explained
  5. Employer reviews profiles and selects candidates to contact
  6. Candidate-employer interviews proceed as normal
  7. Placement outcomes inform model updates
