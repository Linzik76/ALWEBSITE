Frequently Asked Questions

Everything candidates, employers and partners ask us, answered.

For Candidates

How does the AI matching work?

Our matching engine analyzes 30 candidate features (skills, personality, learning style, career goals, etc.) and 12 vacancy features to predict placement success. The model uses XGBoost and explains each match in plain English so you understand why you're matched to a role.

Is ApprenticeLinks free for candidates?

Yes, completely free. You can create a profile, complete the readiness programme, and access matched opportunities without any cost.

How long does the readiness programme take?

The full programme is approximately 20-25 hours. You can complete it at your own pace—most candidates finish in 4-8 weeks.

What if I don't match with any roles?

If you don't have immediate matches, we'll recommend readiness modules to improve your profile, connect you with mentors, and notify you when matching roles become available.

Can I apply to roles manually if I don't want a match?

Yes. While we recommend matches, you can also browse and apply directly to vacancies posted on the platform.

For Employers

How much does ApprenticeLinks cost?

Pricing depends on your plan. Starter (small employers): €20-50/month. Growth: €100-300/month. Enterprise: custom pricing. All plans include access to the matching algorithm and candidate profiles.

How does the matching work for employers?

When you post a vacancy, our AI analyzes your role, company, and culture, then identifies pre-assessed candidates with the highest predicted success probability. You see the top matches with explanations.

How many candidates will I see for a vacancy?

You'll see 5-20 top matches, depending on vacancy and candidate availability. This is intentionally selective—we want quality over quantity.

Can I still use traditional recruitment alongside ApprenticeLinks?

Absolutely. ApprenticeLinks is designed to complement your existing processes, not replace them. Use our matches and still accept direct applications if you prefer.

What data do you collect about my company?

We collect role descriptions, company info, and outcomes data (placements, completions, retention). All data is securely stored and never shared with third parties.

General

When is ApprenticeLinks launching?

We're currently in Phase 0 (manual pilot). Phase 1 MVP launches Q1 2027, with the full AI matching engine (Phase 3) live by Q4 2027.

Will ApprenticeLinks expand to the UK?

Yes. We're launching in Ireland first (2027), then expanding to the UK (2028). The apprenticeship systems are similar, and the same matching approach applies.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use enterprise-grade encryption, comply with GDPR and Irish data protection laws, and undergo regular security audits. Candidate data is never shared with employers without consent.

How do I contact support?

Email: support@apprenticelinks.ie. Or use the contact form on our website. We aim to respond within 24 hours.
