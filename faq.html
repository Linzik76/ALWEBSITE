How does the matching work?

Our matching engine looks at 30 candidate features (skills, personality, learning style, career goals and more) and 12 vacancy features to predict placement success. Each match is explained in plain English so you understand why you have been matched to a role.

Is ApprenticeLinks free for candidates?

Yes, completely free. You can create a profile, complete the Readiness Programme and access matched opportunities at no cost.

How long does the Readiness Programme take?

Around 20–25 hours in total. You can go at your own pace, and most candidates finish in 4–8 weeks.

What if I don't match with any roles?

We will recommend readiness modules to strengthen your profile, connect you with mentors and let you know when matching roles become available.

Can I apply to roles myself?

Yes. We recommend matches, but you can also browse and apply directly to vacancies posted on the platform.

How much does ApprenticeLinks cost?

We offer Starter, Growth and Enterprise plans to suit businesses of every size. All plans include matching and candidate profiles. Get in touch and we will find the right plan for you.

How does matching work for employers?

When you post a vacancy, we analyse your role, company and culture, then identify pre-assessed candidates with the highest predicted success. You see the top matches with explanations.

How many candidates will I see?

Typically 5–20 top matches, depending on the vacancy and candidate availability. It is intentionally selective: quality over quantity.

Can I still use my usual recruitment?

Absolutely. ApprenticeLinks complements your existing process. Use our matches and still accept direct applications if you prefer.

What data do you collect about my company?

Role descriptions, company information and outcomes data (placements, completions, retention). All data is stored securely and never shared with third parties.

When is ApprenticeLinks launching?

We are currently in a manual pilot. The MVP launches in Q1 2027, with the full matching engine live by Q4 2027.

Will ApprenticeLinks expand to the UK?

Yes. We are launching in Ireland first (2027), then expanding to the UK (2028).

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use strong encryption and comply with GDPR and Irish data protection law. Candidate data is never shared with employers without consent.

How do I contact support?

Email support@apprenticelinks.ie or visit our contact page. We aim to respond within 24 hours.

We're happy to help. Drop us a line.
