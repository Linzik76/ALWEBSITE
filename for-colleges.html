Working with ETBs

Support built for Education & Training Boards

The challenge & how we help

The challenge

Candidates can arrive unprepared for the workplace, and placements that are a poor fit can break down early.

How we help

Our Readiness Programme and matching help your learners start apprenticeships prepared, with employers who genuinely suit them.

  • Early identification of readiness gaps
  • Automatic readiness programme access
  • Better-matched placements
  • SOLAS & ETB system integration

What ETBs get

For your learners

Free, ready-made preparation content your learners can work through at their own pace.

For your team

Assessments that show each learner's strengths and where they may need extra support before they apply.

  • Free readiness content for learners
  • Personality & learning style assessments
  • White-label integration available
  • Co-branding opportunities
