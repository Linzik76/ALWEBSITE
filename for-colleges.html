Who we work with

Support built for education providers

ETBs (Education & Training Boards)

The challenge

Candidates arrive unprepared, placements fail, completion rates suffer and data is scattered across systems.

How we help

We integrate with SOLAS and ETB systems to spot at-risk candidates early, provide readiness support and track outcomes in real time.

  • Early identification of readiness gaps
  • Automated readiness programme access
  • Real-time outcome tracking
  • Reduced early termination rates
  • API access for integration

Colleges & Training Providers

The challenge

How do you make sure apprentices are ready for success in the workplace?

How we help

Embed our readiness modules into your programme, use our assessments to find strengths and gaps, and track outcomes after placement.

  • Free readiness content for learners
  • Personality & learning style assessments
  • Outcome tracking by programme
  • White-label integration available
  • Co-branding opportunities
Integration features

Plugs into the systems you already use

Data integration

Connect your learner management system via API. Learners are enrolled in readiness modules automatically on intake.

Outcome tracking

Monitor completion, workplace performance and retention, and compare by programme, sector and employer.

White-label

Customise the platform with your branding, logo and programme-specific content.

Analytics dashboard

Real-time programme metrics, readiness scores and outcome predictions.

Early intervention

Identify and support at-risk candidates before placements break down.

Competitive edge

Stand out with better placement success and data-driven insight into your offering.
