Partner with ApprenticeLinks

ETBs, colleges, and training providers play a critical role in apprenticeship success. ApprenticeLinks partners with education providers to identify candidate readiness gaps, prepare learners, and track outcomes.

For ETBs (Education & Training Boards)

Challenge: Candidates arrive unprepared. Placements fail. Completion rates suffer. Data is fragmented across systems.

Solution: ApprenticeLinks integrates with SOLAS and ETB systems to identify at-risk candidates early, provide readiness support, and track outcomes in real time.

Benefits:

  • Early identification of candidate readiness gaps
  • Automated readiness programme access for your learners
  • Real-time outcome tracking across all placements
  • Data-driven insights on programme effectiveness
  • Reduced early termination rates
  • API access for system integration

For Colleges & Training Providers

Challenge: How do you ensure apprentices are prepared for workplace success?

Solution: Embed ApprenticeLinks readiness modules into your programme. Use our assessments to identify strengths and gaps. Track outcomes post-placement.

Benefits:

  • Free readiness content for your candidates
  • Personality and learning style assessments
  • Outcome tracking to measure programme success
  • White-label integration available
  • Partnership and co-branding opportunities

Integration Features

Data Integration: Connect your learner management system (LMS) to ApprenticeLinks via API. Candidates are automatically enrolled in readiness modules upon intake.

Outcome Tracking: Monitor completion rates, workplace performance, and long-term retention for every apprentice. Compare outcomes by programme, sector, and employer.

White-Label: Customize the platform with your branding, logo, and programme-specific content.

Analytics Dashboard: View real-time programme metrics, readiness scores, and outcome predictions.

Why Partner with ApprenticeLinks?

  • Improve placement success and completion rates
  • Identify and support at-risk candidates early
  • Access free, comprehensive readiness content for learners
  • Track outcomes across all your programmes in one system
  • Increase competitive advantage in the apprenticeship market
  • Gain data-driven insights to improve your offering
