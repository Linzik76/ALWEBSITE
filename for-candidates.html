Find Your Perfect Match

Not all apprenticeships are created equal—and neither are candidates. Our AI-powered matching engine considers your unique skills, interests, personality, learning style, and career goals to connect you with roles where you'll genuinely thrive.

The Candidate Journey

1. Create Your Profile - Tell us about yourself: skills, interests, personality traits (Big Five), and career aspirations.

2. Complete the Readiness Programme - Access 9 comprehensive modules covering CV writing, interview prep, workplace skills, digital literacy, and more.

3. Get Matched - Our algorithm identifies roles perfectly suited to you, with personalized explanations of why each match is a good fit.

4. Apply & Interview - Prepared and confident, you apply to matched positions and ace your interviews.

5. Succeed & Grow - Start your apprenticeship with mentor support, progress tracking, and pathways to Level 4, 5, and 6 qualifications.

The Readiness Programme

We don't just match you—we prepare you. Our 9-module readiness programme covers:

  • Apprenticeship 101 - Understand the landscape and your options
  • CV Development - Craft a compelling CV
  • Interview Prep - Master interview techniques
  • Communication Skills - Ace workplace communication
  • Workplace Readiness - Navigate your first role
  • Digital Skills - Essential tech competencies
  • Employer Research - Know who you're applying to
  • Personal Branding - Stand out from the crowd
  • Soft Skills Assessment - Develop self-awareness

Why ApprenticeLinks for Candidates?

  • Personalized Matching: No spray-and-pray applications—get matched to roles where you'll succeed
  • Preparation: Build confidence with our comprehensive readiness modules
  • Support: Access mentor guidance throughout your apprenticeship
  • Transparency: Understand why you're matched to each role
  • Free Access: Candidates use ApprenticeLinks completely free
