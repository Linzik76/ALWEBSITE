Readiness Programme

9 Modules to Prepare for Success

Our comprehensive readiness programme prepares you for every stage of your apprenticeship journey. Each module includes video content, practical exercises, quizzes, and reflection activities.

Module 1: Apprenticeship 101

Goal: Understand the Irish apprenticeship landscape and your options.

Learn about the 87 apprenticeship programmes, funding, support agencies (SOLAS, ETBs, employers), and pathways to progression. Understand what makes an apprenticeship succeed.

Duration: 2 hours | Outcomes: Understand the landscape; identify your programme interests.

Module 2: CV Development

Goal: Create a compelling CV that stands out.

Learn CV structure, how to highlight transferable skills, quantify achievements, tailor for apprenticeships, and avoid common mistakes. Get feedback on your draft.

Duration: 2.5 hours | Outcomes: Completed, employer-ready CV.

Module 3: Interview Prep

Goal: Master interview techniques and build confidence.

Learn interview types, common questions, STAR method, body language, and how to ask questions. Practice with AI-powered interview simulations.

Duration: 3 hours | Outcomes: Interview confidence; practice with feedback.

Module 4: Communication Skills

Goal: Develop professional communication for the workplace.

Master email etiquette, phone communication, giving and receiving feedback, difficult conversations, and team collaboration.

Duration: 2.5 hours | Outcomes: Professional communication toolkit.

Module 5: Workplace Readiness

Goal: Prepare for your first day and beyond.

Learn workplace culture, dress codes, punctuality, reporting structures, asking for help, and building professional relationships. Understand workplace rights and responsibilities.

Duration: 2 hours | Outcomes: Confidence for day one.

Module 6: Digital Skills

Goal: Master essential digital literacy.

Learn Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Outlook), online collaboration tools, cybersecurity, and digital communication platforms.

Duration: 3 hours | Outcomes: Hands-on digital skills; certifications.

Module 7: Employer Research

Goal: Research employers before applying.

Learn how to research company culture, financials, career progression, and whether an employer is right for you. Find resources and red flags.

Duration: 2 hours | Outcomes: Employer research toolkit.

Module 8: Personal Branding

Goal: Build your professional brand and online presence.

Learn LinkedIn profile optimization, portfolio building, social media etiquette, and how to stand out authentically.

Duration: 2.5 hours | Outcomes: Polished LinkedIn profile; portfolio ready.

Module 9: Soft Skills Assessment

Goal: Develop self-awareness and soft skills.

Reflect on your strengths, development areas, personality (Big Five assessment), learning style, and core values. Build a personal development plan.

Duration: 2 hours | Outcomes: Self-awareness; development plan.
