The modules

Nine steps to being job-ready

Module 1

Apprenticeship 101

Understand the Irish apprenticeship landscape and your options.

Learn about the 87 apprenticeship programmes, funding, support agencies (SOLAS, ETBs, employers) and progression pathways.

⏱ 2 hrs🎯 Know your options and interests
Module 2

CV Development

Create a compelling CV that stands out.

CV structure, transferable skills, tailoring for apprenticeships and avoiding common mistakes, with feedback on your draft.

⏱ 2.5 hrs🎯 An employer-ready CV
Module 3

Interview Prep

Master interview techniques and build confidence.

Interview types, common questions, the STAR method, body language and AI-powered practice interviews.

⏱ 3 hrs🎯 Interview confidence
Module 4

Communication Skills

Develop professional communication for work.

Email etiquette, phone calls, giving and receiving feedback, difficult conversations and teamwork.

⏱ 2.5 hrs🎯 A communication toolkit
Module 5

Workplace Readiness

Prepare for your first day and beyond.

Workplace culture, punctuality, reporting structures, asking for help and your rights and responsibilities.

⏱ 2 hrs🎯 Confidence for day one
Module 6

Digital Skills

Master essential digital literacy.

Word, Excel, Outlook, online collaboration tools, cybersecurity and digital communication.

⏱ 3 hrs🎯 Hands-on digital skills
Module 7

Employer Research

Research employers before applying.

How to research company culture, career progression and whether an employer is right for you, plus red flags.

⏱ 2 hrs🎯 An employer research toolkit
Module 8

Personal Branding

Build your professional brand online.

LinkedIn profiles, portfolios, social media etiquette and how to stand out authentically.

⏱ 2.5 hrs🎯 A polished LinkedIn profile
Module 9

Soft Skills Assessment

Develop self-awareness and soft skills.

Reflect on your strengths, personality (Big Five), learning style and values, and build a development plan.

⏱ 2 hrs🎯 A personal development plan
