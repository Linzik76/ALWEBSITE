The business case

Six reasons apprentices pay off

Cost-effective training

Government subsidies and incentives reduce your training costs while you build talent and cut recruitment spend.

Retention & loyalty

Apprentices you hire and train are far more likely to stay long-term. You build your culture from day one.

Lower turnover costs

Replacing an employee can cost 50–200% of salary. Trained apprentices mean stable, loyal teams.

Productivity gains

Structured apprenticeships with college days improve job readiness compared to unstructured training.

Tax benefits

Training supports and incentives reduce your net cost to hire and develop apprentices.

Skills gaps solved

Build expertise in construction, hospitality, manufacturing, IT and healthcare, where shortages are severe.

Common concerns

We hear you

"I don't have time to mentor."

Apprenticeships pair on-the-job training with structured college learning. Your team provides supervision; the college provides the curriculum. ApprenticeLinks helps with readiness prep and outcome tracking.

"Apprentices aren't productive at first."

There is a ramp-up period. But incentives, reduced training costs and long-term retention more than offset it, and our Readiness Programme speeds up time-to-productivity.

"What if they leave early?"

Well-matched, well-prepared apprentices are far less likely to leave. We identify strong fits and prepare candidates, with the aim of reducing early termination by 30–40%.

"I don't know how to hire apprentices."

We make it easy: post a vacancy, receive matched candidates, interview and hire. We handle readiness, matching and outcome tracking.
