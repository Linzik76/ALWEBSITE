Why Hire Apprentices

Build a loyal, skilled team and close your skills gaps with apprentices trained your way.

I am hiring an apprentice

Build Your Talent Pipeline

Apprenticeships are the fastest-growing talent development pathway in Ireland. Unlike traditional recruitment, apprentices bring eagerness to learn, fresh perspectives, and long-term commitment.

The Business Case for Apprenticeships

1. Cost-Effective Training: Government subsidies and tax incentives reduce your training costs. You're building talent while reducing recruitment spend.

2. Retention & Loyalty: Apprentices hired and trained by you are far more likely to stay long-term than mid-career hires. You build company culture from day one.

3. Reduced Turnover Costs: The average cost to replace an employee is 50-200% of salary. Apprentices, once trained, represent stable, loyal teams.

4. Productivity Gains: Structured apprenticeships (with college days integrated) improve job readiness compared to unstructured on-the-job training.

5. Tax Benefits: Ireland's apprenticeship levy and training tax credits reduce your net cost to hire and develop apprentices.

6. Skill Gaps Solved: Build expertise in critical areas (construction, hospitality, advanced manufacturing, IT, healthcare) where skills shortages are severe.

Industry Growth

2025 Landscape:

  • 31,039 apprentices nationwide
  • 87 apprenticeship programmes across 12 sectors
  • 10,384 employers actively hiring apprentices
  • Fastest-growing pathway post-school

Government Support: SOLAS (State agency) manages the apprenticeship system. ETBs (Education & Training Boards) run training. Government funds 80% of college-based training costs.

What Apprenticeships Cover

Irish apprenticeships are typically 3-4 years and include:

  • On-the-job training (4 days/week) with you
  • College-based instruction (1 day/week) at ETB or provider
  • Technical, practical, and soft skills development
  • Progression to Level 4, 5, or 6 qualifications

Sectors Include: Construction, engineering, hospitality, hairdressing, early childcare, healthcare, IT, advanced manufacturing, financial services, retail, horticulture, and more.

Common Concerns & Solutions

Concern: "I don't have time to mentor."

Solution: Apprenticeships pair on-the-job training with structured college learning. Your team provides supervision; college provides curriculum. ApprenticeLinks helps with readiness prep and outcome tracking.

Concern: "Apprentices aren't productive at first."

Solution: Yes, there's a ramp-up period. But government incentives, reduced training costs, and long-term retention more than offset initial lower productivity. And our readiness programme accelerates time-to-productivity.

Concern: "What if they leave early?"

Solution: Apprentices with good matches and preparation are far less likely to leave. ApprenticeLinks identifies strong fits and prepares candidates, reducing early termination by 30-40%.

Concern: "I don't know how to hire apprentices."

Solution: ApprenticeLinks makes it easy. Post a vacancy, receive matched candidates, interview, hire. We handle readiness, matching, and outcome tracking.

Company Size: 500+ people | Benefit: Build a consistent talent pipeline; reduce recruitment costs; strengthen employer brand as a "training company."

Sector-Specific: Construction, hospitality, advanced manufacturing see immediate skills availability gains.
